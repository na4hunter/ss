# Privacy Policy for Simple Signboard

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Thank you for using **Simple Signboard**.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Simple Signboard mobile application (the “App”) handles information when you use it.

## 1. Overview

Simple Signboard is a simple mobile application designed to display text or images on the screen for meeting guests, for example in airports, stations, hotels, or other public places.

The App is designed to work **offline** and does **not require user registration** to use its main features.

## 2. Information We Collect

Simple Signboard is designed to collect as little information as possible.

### Information stored locally on your device
The App may store the following information **only on your device**:

- selected theme settings
- selected language
- preferred text size
- preferred display mode such as text or image
- text entered by the user if the application saves the last used value locally

This information is used only to make the App work properly and improve your experience.

## 3. Images Selected by the User

If you choose the image mode, the App may request access to photos or files on your device so you can select an image.

Simple Signboard does **not upload, transmit, or sell** your images. Selected images are used only for display inside the App, unless you explicitly share content using your device’s built in sharing features.

## 4. Internet and Offline Use

The main features of Simple Signboard work **without an internet connection**.

The App does not require an online account and does not depend on a backend server for its core functionality.

Some optional features, such as opening external links, app store pages, privacy policy pages hosted online, or sharing through third party apps, may require internet access.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data.

We do **not** share your personal data with third parties, except in the following limited cases:

- when required by law
- when you intentionally use external sharing features available on your device
- when a third party platform such as an app store or operating system processes data under its own privacy policy

## 6. Third Party Services

The App may use third party libraries or system services for functionality such as:

- local settings storage
- image selection from device storage
- sharing content or app links
- opening external links

These services may be provided by your mobile operating system, device manufacturer, or third party providers. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 7. Personal Data

Simple Signboard is not intended to collect sensitive personal data.

The App does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment information in order to use its basic features.

If you voluntarily contact us, any information you provide in that communication will be used only to respond to your request.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Simple Signboard is not directed specifically to children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App simple and secure.

Because most settings are stored locally on your device, you are also responsible for protecting access to your phone or tablet.

## 10. Data Retention

Locally stored settings remain on your device until:

- you change them
- you clear the app data
- you uninstall the App

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights related to your personal data under applicable law.

Because Simple Signboard generally does not collect personal data on its own servers, many of these rights may be exercised by managing the App directly on your device, including by deleting stored app data or uninstalling the App.

## 12. External Links

The App may contain links to external pages or other applications, such as:

- privacy policy pages
- app store listings
- other apps
- sharing destinations chosen by the user

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third party websites, stores, or applications.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document.
